CAUSE AND IMPLEMENTATION

1. Tetrazole / azole radical location was treated as a hydrogen attachment site.
   In the four gen_eed3 raw forms, this created both different N-H tautomers and
   an artificial non-aromatic 5H tetrazole when the radical was on ring carbon.
   Calling a tautomer canonicalizer only after this H cap cannot restore that
   5H form with RDKit's default rules.

   New order: parse without full sanitization; assign valence/radicals/rings;
   restore the recognised azolate electronic representation; cap remaining
   radicals; sanitize; uncharge; enumerate and select the canonical tautomer.
   The ring repair requires an isolated five-member C/N ring, two double bonds,
   exactly one radical electron, and no existing N-H/N-substitution or exocyclic
   multiple bond. Radical positions are removed before canonical atom ranking
   chooses a reproducible N anion representation. The subsequent canonical
   tautomer rule reconciles the supported N-H forms.
   Unresolved C/N five-ring radical patterns are rejected explicitly.

2. The supplied gen_2aed evidence contains [O][C]([O])R.
   The old all-radical H cap made R-CH(OH)2, adding two excess hydrogens relative
   to the intended carboxylic acid. The new matched C/O multiradical rule first
   restores a carbonyl/carboxylate; neutralization then produces R-C(=O)OH.
   This recognises a specific MOFid extraction artefact, not a general rewrite
   of legitimate geminal diols. In the supplied gen_2aed example the original
   four apparent fragment identities reduce to two corrected parent identities.

3. Some malformed aromatic azolates were rejected by the upstream full-sanitize
   parser before standardization. The driver now reads raw linker_stats.txt
   column 6 directly. A matched C/N five-ring with both an existing pyrrolic
   donor and a surplus [n-] is repaired before kekulization. Valid [n-] azolates
   remain available to the Uncharger; no blanket textual [n-] replacement is used.

4. Failed fragments could disappear while SA/HOMO maxima were still reported.
   Every raw fragment, repair, exclusion and failure now appears in
   WORK/fragment_standardization.json. Any failed eligible fragment or malformed
   row makes the organic set incomplete and stops the descriptor workflow with
   the audit path. The existing element allow-list is retained; excluded
   metal/dummy components are listed explicitly. This definition does not certify
   a chemical linker role for every extracted fragment.
   SA is defined only if every standardized parent scores. Channel B remains the
   max over the complete standardized parent set, and non-finite HOMOs count as
   failures. No maximum over a silently shortened eligible set is accepted.

5. Canonical atom order, explicit-H handling and removal of atom-map annotations
   are shared by SA and HOMO. Isotopes and permanent charges are preserved.
   isomeric=False removes stereochemistry while retaining isotopes. RDKit's
   tautomer transformations may remove stereochemistry at participating sites;
   unaffected chiral centres and tested E/Z bonds are retained by default.

6. QM audit metadata used the first energy summary in xTB --opt output, which
   is the initial geometry's energy. parse_total_energy now reads the last
   summary. This corrects opt_energy_hartree metadata; the HOMO is still taken
   from the separate single-point calculation at the optimized geometry.

VERIFICATION

Environment: Python 3.13, RDKit 2026.03.6; xTB 6.7.1 (edcfbbe), one thread.
Embedding protocol unchanged: ETKDGv3 seed 0xC0FFEE, 16 conformers, MMFF/UFF
prescreen, GFN2-xTB SP -> optimization -> SP. xTB binary SHA256 and individual
run records are saved in validation/linkerfix/qm/verification.json.

A. validation/test_linker_postprocess.py: 11 test groups PASS.
   Eighteen linker families cover carboxylic acids, imidazole, pyrazole, triazoles,
   tetrazole, pyrrole, benzimidazole, pyridone, pyridine, phenol, thiophenol,
   aniline and sulfonic acid. Checks also cover N-oxide, nitro, permanent N+,
   N-substituted azole, isotopes, atom maps, explicit H, chirality/E-Z, malformed
   inputs, incomplete enumeration, complete aggregation, parser audit output,
   and the final xTB optimization energy. The four tetrazole inputs plus the
   18 families each undergo 20 shuffled atom-order checks: 440 cases total.

B. All four raw gen_eed3 forms produce exactly:
   c1ccc2c(-c3nn[nH]n3)cc(-c3nn[nH]n3)c-2cc1
   SA score: 2.4680365646222064; one shared QM identity key: 7a64892fd307f390.
   Two independent, fresh xTB runs from raw A and raw D converge to HOMO -9.9582 eV
   in both runs. Embedded XYZ, optimized XYZ and final SP XYZ are byte-identical.
   All six checks in validation/linkerfix/qm/verification.json PASS.
   The atom-order and QM checks establish this tested convention's consistency;
   they do not identify the physical lowest-energy tetrazole tautomer.

C. Real-data audit: 1,647 distinct raw strings sampled from a 76,779-row local
   catalogue, including frequency/motif strata, a seeded random sample, and all
   489 strings in the historical unparsable-fragment table. Seed 20261024.
   1,482 accepted; each passed idempotence and one shuffled atom-order check.
   No invariance violations were observed. Of the 489 old failures, 409 now yield
   a parent. Of all 1,647 samples, 727 outputs/statuses change versus the original.
   This is a deliberately enriched sample, NOT a population incidence estimate.

   The 165 explicit failures are retained in real_fragment_audit.csv:
     85 incomplete tautomer enumerations (68 max-transforms, 17 max-tautomers),
     45 invalid N valence cases,
     18 unsupported azole radical patterns,
     12 disconnected fragments,
      5 unsupported elements or attachment dummies.
   All 85 previously valued strings newly rejected here hit an enumeration limit.
   The enumerator's fixed bounds are 1,000 tautomers and 1,000 transforms.
   Large multi-site molecules may exceed them. Their canonical parent is left
   undefined rather than chosen from an order-dependent partial enumeration.
   No global coverage or chemical correctness for every accepted sample is claimed.

D. Source verification: original ZIP checksum matches SHA256SUMS. Only the three
   source files plus README.txt and VERSION differ from the archive; every other
   original file, including baseline/, remains byte-identical. The patch is
   checked against pristine source files. The corrected package has its own hash.

copy. --baseline deliberately retains the original uncorrected chemistry.

BOUNDARIES

Always restart from RAW MOFid linker_stats.txt. Previously H-capped 5H strings
have lost the radical evidence; this patch does not reconstruct that evidence
from historical standardized SMILES. Canonical tautomer selection is an
operational descriptor definition, not solution speciation or an energy search.
It is version-dependent, so the RDKit version and standardizer ID are recorded.
The implementation uses RDKit's documented enumeration/status/canonical-choice API:
https://rdkit.org/docs/source/rdkit.Chem.MolStandardize.rdMolStandardize.html

No MOFid C++ changes, full-CIF repeat decomposition, GPU Ehull calculation,
end-to-end five-feature model evaluation, population reprocessing, training,
rescoring, calibration, threshold or pi_U change was executed. Raw CIFs,
fixed-data tables/matrices, frozen models and historical scores are untouched.
Affected training features would need a separately scoped rebuild before a model
could be validated under this revised feature convention. Original 3.1 timing
and numerical-equivalence claims are historical evidence only.
